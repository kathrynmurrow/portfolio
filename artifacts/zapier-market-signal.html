The Challenge

The market moves faster than any résumé.

I work at the intersection of learning systems, organizational effectiveness, and AI-enabled operations. That means the roles most aligned with where I'm headed — GTM Enablement, Automations Strategy, OD&E — are evolving constantly. New titles emerge. Skill expectations shift. What's in demand this quarter may be table stakes by next year.

Staying relevant means understanding that landscape continuously, not just when it's time to update a résumé. I need to know what skills are being asked for, what problems companies are trying to solve, and how my current work maps to where high-value opportunities are heading.

So I built a system that does the market research for me — automatically, every morning — so I can use that intelligence to guide my growth, not just react to it.

🧭 Without a System

  • Market signals are scattered across platforms with no easy way to synthesize them
  • Skill trends shift quietly — you don't notice until you're behind
  • The same noise repeats; it's hard to spot what's genuinely new
  • No centralized view of what the market is asking for right now
  • Adjacent roles and growth areas are hard to track across different title conventions
  • Development decisions get made on intuition instead of real market data

Step by Step

Breaking it down.

01
Schedule by Zapier

The Daily Trigger

Every morning at 8 AM, the Zap fires automatically. No manual effort, no remembering to check — just a consistent, daily market pulse. I included weekends because the market doesn't pause, and neither should my awareness of it.

02
Web Search by Zapier

The Market Signal Query

The search query is where the intelligence lives. Using Boolean logic, I track multiple role title variations across the capability areas most relevant to my growth trajectory — enablement operations, AI-powered learning, systems architecture, organizational effectiveness. Salary ranges help filter for senior-level market positioning so I'm reading signals at the right level.

// Tracking capability areas that matter to my growth ((operations enablement OR enablement operations OR learning operations OR AI enablement OR systems architecture) AND (remote OR "work from home") AND ("$130" OR "$140" OR "$150")) site:linkedin.com OR site:edtech.com
03
Looping by Zapier

Processing Each Signal

Web Search returns up to 15 results as a batch. The Loop step unpacks each one for individual processing — deduplication, logging, and notification. Each iteration carries the role title, URL, and description snippet forward as discrete data for analysis.

04
Google Sheets

Deduplication via URL Lookup

Each result URL is checked against my tracker — if it already exists, the Zap stops for that iteration. This keeps the signal clean. I'm only seeing genuinely new market data each morning, not the same noise recycled across days.

05
Filter by Zapier

The Gatekeeper

A Filter step checks whether the lookup returned a match. If the role is already tracked, the Zap stops. If it's new, it continues. This single step ensures the entire downstream pipeline only runs on net-new market intelligence.

// Filter condition Data was found = False // → Only new market signals pass through
06
Google Sheets

Logging to the Intelligence Tracker

New signals are written to a structured Google Sheet: date captured, role title, source link, salary range, remote status, and a notes column I use to tag what skill themes or emerging patterns each role reflects. Over time, this becomes a longitudinal view of where the market is heading — not just a snapshot.

07
Email by Zapier

The Morning Briefing

Each new signal lands in my inbox as a digest — role title, source, and why it's relevant to the capability areas I'm tracking. Skimmable by design. I review it the way I'd review any professional development input: with the question what does this tell me about where I should be investing?

Make It Yours

Adapt it to your growth trajectory.

Map Your Capability Areas

Replace the keywords with the skill clusters and role types most relevant to where you want to grow. Stack synonyms — the market doesn't use consistent language, and neither should your query.

Calibrate the Level Signal

Salary markers aren't about targeting pay — they're about reading the right tier of the market. Set them to match the seniority level where you want to understand skill expectations.

Choose Your Signal Sources

Different industries live on different platforms. Add or swap site: operators to pull from the job boards, communities, or talent networks most active in your space.

Add AI-Powered Synthesis

Drop an "AI by Zapier" step after the filter to auto-extract the top 3 skills mentioned, tag capability themes, or score relevance to your development priorities before it hits your Sheets log.
