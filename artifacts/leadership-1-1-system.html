1:1 Leadership System

Structured weekly coaching cadence designed to drive performance, accountability, and leader development.

Performance Review (10–15 min)

Review key metrics and trends from the past week. Identify wins, gaps in execution, and any unexpected shifts in performance.

Root Cause Discussion (10–15 min)

Explore what is driving current performance. Identify patterns, underlying issues, and areas that have not yet been addressed.

Leader Development Focus (15–20 min)

Coach on leadership behaviors, identify development gaps, and practice real-world coaching or decision-making scenarios.

Operational Priorities (10–15 min)

Align on upcoming business priorities, clarify expectations, and identify risks before execution begins.

Commitments & Follow-Up (5–10 min)

Define clear next actions, confirm accountability, and set the focus for the next check-in.

This system is designed to move 1:1s from status updates to structured performance and development conversations that drive measurable behavior change.

Why This System Works

  • Shifts conversations from reactive to proactive
  • Aligns performance, behavior, and execution in one cadence
  • Creates consistency across leaders and locations
