Support Menu Library

Choose the right support instead of defaulting to retraining or vague feedback.

Clarify expectation
Use when the person may not know what good looks like. Evidence: can restate expectation.
Model behavior
Use when they need to see the skill in context. Evidence: can explain what worked.
Guided practice
Use when they understand but need reps. Evidence: attempts behavior with reduced prompting.
Job aid / checklist
Use when consistency or memory load is the barrier. Evidence: fewer missed steps.
Stretch assignment
Use when readiness evidence is emerging. Evidence: performs next-level behavior safely.
Accountability conversation
Use after clarity, support, follow-up, and repeated evidence.
