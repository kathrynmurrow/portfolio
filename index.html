Why It Matters
01 — The Problem

Investment without return

Organizations were investing heavily in training and initiatives, but performance gaps remained. Leaders lacked clarity on root cause, resulting in misaligned solutions and inconsistent execution across hundreds of locations.

02 — The Insight

The issue was diagnosis, not training

Without a structured method to identify what was actually driving performance gaps, even well-designed solutions failed. The gap wasn't capability — it was clarity.

03 — The System Built

A connected enablement ecosystem

Designed end-to-end: beginning with diagnosis, defining governance, measuring outcomes, driving execution, and scaling through AI.

  • Diagnosis system to identify root causes before solution deployment
  • Governance system to define ownership and capability standards
  • Dashboards to measure real-time performance across all locations
  • Execution systems to drive consistent behavior at scale
  • AI layer to scale decision-making and reduce manual effort
04 — The Impact

Measurable at enterprise scale

  • Improved decision accuracy across leadership levels
  • Reduced wasted investment in ineffective initiatives
  • Real-time visibility across 870+ locations and 15K+ employees
  • Scalable system for ongoing, sustainable performance improvement
05 — Why It Matters

Beyond fragmented solutions

This work demonstrates how organizations can move beyond one-off interventions and build systems that consistently translate strategy into measurable execution — at any scale.

GrowthLens

Most performance development systems capture moments. GrowthLens builds a continuous, AI-interpreted record of growth over time.

An inaugural performance development and coaching intelligence system — designed to give leaders a real-time, AI-powered view of each person's growth trajectory. Not documentation. Not annual reviews. A living, evidence-based story of development that compounds over time and informs every coaching conversation.

The gap it fills: The performance development industry runs on snapshots — annual reviews, one-off check-ins, recency-biased feedback.
