How I Work

The Process Behind
the Results

01

Diagnose the system

I start before any content is created. What's actually causing the gap? Observation, data, and structured diagnostics. Not a single slide gets built until that question has a real answer.

02

Map the cascade

Who needs to know what, to do what, by when, and who verifies it? I map the full chain from VP to frontline before anything gets built.

03

Build with data loops

Every solution has a measurement layer designed in from the start. Pre-work pulse checks, exit tickets, 30/60/90 follow-up. Not bolted on after the fact.

04

Scale the system

AI tooling, documented governance, LMS infrastructure. Everything is designed to replicate without manual intervention and to keep running after I move on to the next problem.
